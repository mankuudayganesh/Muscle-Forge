7-Day Fat Loss Meal Plan Under ₹1500

📅 January 2024 | 📖 7 min read | 🔥 Weight Loss

Losing weight doesn't mean expensive diet foods or supplements. Here's a complete 7-day fat loss meal plan that costs less than ₹1500 per week.

The Science Behind Fat Loss

Fat loss requires a calorie deficit - consuming fewer calories than your body burns. Our plan creates a 400-500 calorie daily deficit while keeping protein high to preserve muscle.

🥚 Eggs (30) - ₹150
🍗 Chicken Breast (2kg) - ₹580
🥛 Milk (3 liters) - ₹180
🍚 Brown Rice (1kg) - ₹80
🥬 Vegetables mix (2kg) - ₹100
🍎 Fruits (1kg) - ₹120
🥜 Peanuts (500g) - ₹50

Sample Day Meal Plan

Breakfast (7 AM): Oats with milk + 1 apple (₹35)
Lunch (1 PM): 200g chicken breast + brown rice + vegetables (₹80)
Evening Snack (5 PM): 30g peanuts + green tea (₹15)
Dinner (8 PM): 3 egg whites + vegetable salad (₹25)
Total Daily: ₹155 | Calories: ~1650 | Protein: ~120g

Weekly Meal Rotation

Monday: Chicken + Brown Rice
Tuesday: Paneer + Roti + Salad
Wednesday: Fish + Vegetables
Thursday: Eggs + Lentils
Friday: Chicken + Quinoa
Saturday: Paneer + Brown Rice
Sunday: Eggs + Chicken (refeed day)

Fat Loss Tips

1. Drink 3-4 liters of water daily
2. Walk 8000-10000 steps daily
3. Eat protein with every meal
4. Sleep 7-8 hours
5. No liquid calories (soda, juice, alcohol)

Follow this plan consistently for 4 weeks and expect to lose 2-4 kg of pure fat!
