10 Gym Mistakes Killing Your Gains

📅 January 2024 | 📖 6 min read | ⚠️ Mistakes

Are you working out hard but not seeing results? You might be making these common gym mistakes.

❌ Mistake #1: Poor Form
Using momentum instead of controlled movement. Fix: Lower weight, focus on form, watch tutorial videos.
❌ Mistake #2: Not Progressively Overloading
Lifting the same weight for months. Fix: Add weight, reps, or sets every 1-2 weeks.
❌ Mistake #3: Skipping Leg Day
Leg workouts boost testosterone and overall growth. Fix: Train legs at least once weekly.
❌ Mistake #4: Too Much Cardio
Excessive cardio burns muscle. Fix: Limit cardio to 3x/week for muscle gain goals.
❌ Mistake #5: Not Eating Enough Protein
Muscle needs protein to repair and grow. Fix: Eat 1.6-2.2g protein per kg body weight.
❌ Mistake #6: Poor Sleep
Muscle grows when you rest. Fix: Get 7-8 hours quality sleep nightly.
❌ Mistake #7: Overtraining
No rest days = no recovery. Fix: Take 1-2 rest days weekly.
❌ Mistake #8: Inconsistent Workouts
Skipping workouts ruins progress. Fix: Schedule workouts like appointments.
❌ Mistake #9: Not Tracking Progress
No logs = no improvement. Fix: Track weights, reps, measurements, photos.
❌ Mistake #10: Comparing to Others
Everyone's journey is different. Fix: Focus on YOUR progress, not influencers.

Fix these mistakes and watch your gains skyrocket!
