Complete Protein Guide for Indians

📅 January 2024 | 📖 8 min read | 🥗 Nutrition

Protein is essential for muscle growth, fat loss, and overall health. Here's everything you need to know about protein sources available in India.

Why Protein Matters

For muscle gain: 1.6-2.2g per kg body weight
For fat loss: 1.8-2.4g per kg body weight
For maintenance: 1.2-1.6g per kg body weight

Sample Daily Protein Plan (150g protein)

Breakfast: 4 eggs + 2 bread (30g protein) - ₹30
Lunch: 200g chicken breast (60g protein) - ₹60
Snack: 50g peanuts + 200g curd (20g protein) - ₹25
Dinner: 200g paneer + vegetables (40g protein) - ₹70
Total protein: 150g | Total cost: ₹185

Vegetarian Protein Sources

Paneer, Soya Chunks, Lentils, Chickpeas, Kidney Beans, Greek Yogurt, Milk, Peanuts, Almonds, Quinoa, Amaranth, Seeds (pumpkin, chia, flax).

Non-Vegetarian Protein Sources

Chicken, Eggs, Fish, Mutton, Prawns, Tuna, Whey Protein (if affordable).

Remember: Spread your protein intake across 4-5 meals for maximum muscle protein synthesis!
