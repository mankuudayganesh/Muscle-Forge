Proper Form for Squats & Deadlifts

📅 January 2024 | 📖 5 min read | 📝 Tutorial

How to Squat Correctly

Setup: Bar on upper back (not neck), feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
Descent: Push hips back, keep chest up, back straight. Go to parallel or below.
Ascent: Drive through heels, push hips forward, keep core tight.
Common mistakes: Knees caving in, rounding back, heels lifting off ground.

How to Deadlift Correctly

Setup: Bar over mid-foot, shins touch bar, back straight, shoulders over bar.
Pull: Drive through heels, keep back flat, bar close to body.
Lockout: Push hips through, squeeze glutes, don't hyperextend back.
Common mistakes: Rounded back, jerking the bar, using arms to lift.

Warm-up for Heavy Lifts

1. 5-10 min light cardio
2. Dynamic stretches (leg swings, hip circles)
3. 2-3 warm-up sets with light weight
4. Build up to working weight gradually

Safety Tips

Use a spotter for heavy squats
Learn to fail safely (safety bars, bumper plates)
Wear flat shoes (Chuck Taylors) for deadlifts
Use a belt for sets above 80% of max
