How to Gain Muscle on a Budget

📅 January 2024 | 📖 5 min read | 💪 Muscle Gain

Building muscle doesn't have to cost a fortune. In fact, some of the most effective muscle-building strategies are completely free or very low cost.

1. Focus on Compound Exercises

Compound movements like squats, deadlifts, bench presses, and pull-ups work multiple muscle groups simultaneously. They give you more bang for your buck and don't require expensive equipment.

2. Prioritize Protein-Rich Budget Foods

Instead of expensive supplements, focus on whole foods like eggs (₹5-6 each), paneer (₹350/kg), chicken breast (₹290/kg), lentils, and soya chunks. These provide high-quality protein at a fraction of the cost of supplements.

3. Progressive Overload is Free

You don't need fancy equipment to progressively overload. Add reps, reduce rest time, or improve form - all free strategies that drive muscle growth.

4. Sleep & Recovery Cost Nothing

Muscle grows when you rest, not when you train. Ensure 7-8 hours of quality sleep and take rest days seriously.

Sample Budget Muscle Gain Meal Plan (₹300/day)

Breakfast: 4 eggs + 2 bread slices (₹30)
Lunch: 200g chicken + rice + veggies (₹80)
Evening: 30g peanuts + banana (₹15)
Post-workout: 250ml milk + 2 scoops soya (₹25)
Dinner: 200g paneer + roti + salad (₹150)

Total: ₹300/day - ₹2100/week - Achieve your muscle goals without breaking the bank!
