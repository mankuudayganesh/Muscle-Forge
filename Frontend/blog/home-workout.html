No Equipment Home Workout

📅 January 2024 | 📖 6 min read | 🏠 Home Workout

No gym? No equipment? No problem! Build muscle and burn fat with this effective home workout routine.

Full Body Home Workout (45 minutes)

  • 1. Bodyweight Squats - 4 sets × 15 reps
    Target: Legs, Glutes. Keep chest up, squat to parallel.
  • 2. Push-ups - 4 sets × 10-15 reps
    Target: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps. Modify on knees if needed.
  • 3. Lunges - 3 sets × 12 each leg
    Target: Legs, Balance. Step forward, lower back knee.
  • 4. Plank - 3 sets × 30-60 seconds
    Target: Core. Keep body straight, engage abs.
  • 5. Jumping Jacks - 3 sets × 30 seconds
    Target: Cardio, Full body. Great for warm-up and fat burn.
  • 6. Tricep Dips (using chair) - 3 sets × 10-12 reps
    Target: Triceps. Keep shoulders down, elbows back.
  • 7. Mountain Climbers - 3 sets × 20 reps
    Target: Cardio, Core, Shoulders.
  • 8. Glute Bridges - 3 sets × 15 reps
    Target: Glutes, Hamstrings. Squeeze at top.

Weekly Schedule

Monday: Full Body Workout
Tuesday: 30 min Walking + Abs
Wednesday: Full Body Workout
Thursday: Rest / Light Stretching
Friday: Full Body Workout
Saturday: 45 min Cardio (jumping jacks, run in place)
Sunday: Active Recovery (walking, stretching)

Progression Tips

Week 1-2: Start with 2 sets of each exercise
Week 3-4: Increase to 3 sets
Week 5-6: Add more reps or decrease rest time
Week 7-8: Try advanced variations (plyo pushups, pistol squats)

Stay consistent for 8 weeks and you'll see noticeable changes in your physique!
