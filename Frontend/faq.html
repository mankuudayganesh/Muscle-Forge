Frequently Asked Questions

How does the plan generation work? +

Our AI-powered system analyzes your body metrics (age, weight, height), fitness goals, dietary preference, and weekly budget to create personalized workout and diet plans tailored specifically for you.

Is Muscle Forge free to use? +

Basic plan generation is completely free! You can generate personalized workout and diet plans at no cost. Premium features are available for advanced tracking and analysis.

How accurate are the calorie and protein calculations? +

Our calculators use scientifically validated formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR) and adjust based on your activity level and goals. Results are highly accurate for most individuals.

Can I get a plan for a specific budget? +

Yes! We specialize in budget-smart plans. You enter your weekly food budget, and our system creates diet plans that fit within your budget while meeting your nutritional needs.

What's the 30-Day Transformation Challenge? +

Our gamified challenge lets you earn points for completing daily workouts and tasks. Redeem points for rewards like premium meal plans, training sessions, and merchandise. Complete all 30 days to unlock free rewards!

Are the workouts suitable for beginners? +

Yes! Our plans adjust based on your experience level (beginner, intermediate, advanced). Beginners get simpler exercises with lower volume and bodyweight options.

Can I use Muscle Forge for weight loss? +

Definitely! Select "Fat Loss" as your goal, and we'll create a calorie deficit plan with adequate protein to preserve muscle while losing fat.

Is my data secure? +

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmission. Your personal information is never shared with third parties without your consent.
