Exercise Database

Complete guide to exercises with proper form, target muscles, and instructions

💪 Chest Exercises

Barbell Bench Press
🎯 Target: Chest, Triceps, Front Delts
Lie flat on bench, grip bar shoulder-width, lower to chest, press up explosively.
Incline Dumbbell Press
🎯 Target: Upper Chest
Set bench to 30-45°, press dumbbells up from chest level.
Push Ups
🎯 Target: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
Keep core tight, body straight, lower chest to floor.
Dumbbell Flyes
🎯 Target: Chest
Arms slightly bent, open wide, squeeze at top.

🔥 Back Exercises

Deadlift
🎯 Target: Lower Back, Hamstrings, Glutes
Hinge at hips, flat back, drive through heels.
Pull Ups
🎯 Target: Lats, Biceps
Dead hang, pull elbows to hips, chin above bar.
Lat Pulldown
🎯 Target: Lats
Pull bar to upper chest, full stretch at top.
Seated Cable Row
🎯 Target: Mid Back
Sit tall, pull to navel, squeeze shoulder blades.

🦵 Legs Exercises

Barbell Squat
🎯 Target: Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings
Squat to parallel, drive knees out.
Leg Press
🎯 Target: Quads, Glutes
Lower until 90° knee angle, press through heels.
Walking Lunges
🎯 Target: Quads, Glutes
Step forward, lower back knee to floor.

🏋️ Shoulders Exercises

Dumbbell Shoulder Press
🎯 Target: Shoulders
Press dumbbells overhead, lower to ear height.
Lateral Raise
🎯 Target: Side Delts
Raise to shoulder height, pinkies slightly up.

💪 Arms Exercises (Biceps & Triceps)

Barbell Curl
🎯 Target: Biceps
Elbows pinned, curl to chin, squeeze at top.
Tricep Pushdown
🎯 Target: Triceps
Elbows pinned, push bar down to lockout.
Hammer Curl
🎯 Target: Brachialis, Biceps
Neutral grip throughout.

🎯 Core Exercises

Crunches
🎯 Target: Upper Abs
Curl upper back off floor.
Plank
🎯 Target: Core
Keep body straight, engage core.
Leg Raises
🎯 Target: Lower Abs
Raise legs to 90°, lower slowly.

❤️ Cardio Exercises

Treadmill Running
🎯 Target: Cardiovascular Health
Start comfortable pace, increase gradually.
Jump Rope
🎯 Target: Cardio, Coordination
Land softly on balls of feet.
Cycling
🎯 Target: Lower Body Endurance
Keep steady pace, maintain posture.
